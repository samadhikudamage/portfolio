Mobile App Design

Hela

Project Overview

Hela is a comprehensive mobile app designed to enhance the experience of global travelers. Created specifically for the tourism industry, Hela acts as a personalized travel assistant streamlining every step of a tourist's journey. From planning detailed itineraries and guiding tours, to booking hotels and discovering local blogs, Hela offers a suite of essential features tailored to the needs of foreign visitors. The app was thoughtfully designed to simplify travel logistics and empower users to explore new destinations with ease and confidence.

Problem Statement

Traveling to a new country is exciting but the experience is often fragmented and stressful. Tourists are forced to juggle multiple apps and websites to plan their itinerary, book services, navigate unfamiliar places, and many more. While there are countless tools for individual travel tasks, there's no single, unified platform that brings everything together in one seamless, user-friendly experience. Travelers need an all-in-one solution that simplifies every step of their journey and empowers them to explore with confidence.

Solution — Hela

To address the fragmented and stressful nature of international travel, we created Hela — a comprehensive, all-in-one travel assistant designed to simplify every stage of a tourist's journey. Hela combines essential travel tools into a single, seamless mobile and web platform, eliminating the need for users to juggle multiple apps and websites.

Key Features

  • ✅ Unified Experience: All essential travel tools integrated into one platform
  • ✅ Personalization: Tailored recommendations based on individual preferences
  • ✅ Companion Website: Responsive website for desktop planning
  • ✅ Intuitive Design: Simple, visually engaging, and accessible interface
  • ✅ Stress Reduction: Consolidates fragmented tasks into one cohesive experience

Design Process

As the UI/UX Specialist for Hela, I was involved in every stage of the product design process — from uncovering user needs to delivering intuitive, high-fidelity interfaces. My approach followed a user-centered, iterative design methodology to ensure the final product was both functional and delightful to use.

1. Research & Discovery

Led comprehensive research efforts including user interviews with both frequent and first-time travelers, competitor analysis, and development of detailed user personas based on research findings.

2. Ideation & Information Architecture

Mapped out user flows to visualize key journeys within the app and structured the information architecture to ensure intuitive navigation and logical grouping of features.

3. Wireframing & Low-Fidelity Prototyping

Created low-fidelity wireframes to outline structure and core functionality, focusing on simplifying complex travel tasks into clear, digestible steps.

4. High-Fidelity Design & Interactive Prototypes

Developed polished, visually appealing screens aligned with a modern, accessible design system. Created interactive prototypes using Figma to simulate real user interactions.

5. Developer Collaboration & Design Handoff

Worked closely with the development team providing detailed design specifications, participating in regular design reviews, and addressing technical constraints while maintaining design integrity.

6. Usability Testing & Iteration

Facilitated usability testing sessions to validate design decisions, identify friction points, and collaborate with the team to iterate on designs based on feedback and performance insights.
