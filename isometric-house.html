Mobile App Design

Hela

Project Overview

Hela is a comprehensive mobile app designed to enhance the experience of global travelers. Created specifically for the tourism industry, Hela acts as a personalized travel assistant streamlining every step of a tourist's journey.

Client Tourism Industry Project
Category
Mobile App UI/UX Design Travel
Duration 3 months
Tools Used Figma, Adobe Creative Suite, Principle
Year 2024
View Prototype

To solve the disjointed and often overwhelming experience of international travel, we built Hela a unified travel assistant that brings together everything a tourist needs in one place. From planning and bookings to cultural insights and real-time guidance, Hela offers a seamless, all-in-one platform across both mobile and web. By eliminating the need to switch between multiple tools and apps, it empowers travellers to explore with clarity, confidence, and ease.

Key Features

  • ✅ Unified Experience: All essential travel tools integrated into one platform
  • ✅ Personalization: Tailored recommendations based on individual preferences
  • ✅ Companion Website: Responsive website for desktop planning
  • ✅ Intuitive Design: Simple, visually engaging, and accessible interface
  • ✅ Stress Reduction: Consolidates fragmented tasks into one cohesive experience

Design Process

As the UI/UX Specialist for Hela, I was involved in every stage of the product design process from uncovering user needs to delivering intuitive, high-fidelity interfaces. My approach followed a user-centered, iterative design methodology to ensure the final product was both functional and delightful to use.

2. Ideation & Information Architecture

At this stage, I focused on translating research insights into structured user journeys. I began by mapping out detailed user flows to visualize the end-to-end experience for each core task including itinerary planning, blog exploration, event creation, and more. These flows helped identify the most efficient paths for users to complete actions with minimal friction.

Using these flows as a foundation, I then developed the information architecture, ensuring content and features were grouped logically and navigable across both mobile and web platforms. Special attention was given to creating a structure that could scale with future features such as AI trip suggestions or account personalization while keeping the current MVP experience clean and intuitive.

This stage also involved low-level ideation sessions, where I explored alternative layouts, navigation models, and hierarchy structures, balancing business priorities with user needs. The result was a well-organized system that laid the groundwork for both usability and long-term platform growth.

3. Wireframing & Low-Fidelity Prototyping

At this stage, I focused on translating initial ideas into tangible layouts by creating low-fidelity wireframes for both the mobile app and web platform. These wireframes served as the foundation for visualizing the core structure, navigation, and functionality of key features such as itinerary planning, blog exploration, and event creation.

The goal was to break down complex travel-related tasks into simple, intuitive, and task-focused user flows. By keeping the wireframes intentionally minimal, I was able to prioritize usability, test early concepts with stakeholders, and quickly iterate on layout and logic before moving into high-fidelity design. This phase played a critical role in validating the product direction and ensuring a frictionless experience across devices.

4. High-Fidelity Design & Interactive Prototypes

Building on insights gathered from the wireframing stage, I transitioned into developing high-fidelity screens that reflected the final visual direction of the product. These designs were crafted to be both aesthetically engaging and functionally accessible, ensuring a consistent experience across both mobile and web platforms.

Following several rounds of feedback from stakeholders during the wireframing phase, I continually iterated on layout, content hierarchy, and interaction patterns to align the UI with both user needs and business goals. This collaborative feedback loop helped refine edge cases, improve usability, and strengthen the overall design coherence.

Using Figma, I created fully interactive prototypes that simulated real user journeys including itinerary planning, blog discovery, and event submission. These prototypes served not only as a tool for stakeholder review and usability testing but also as a clear, hands-on reference for the development team, ensuring a smooth design-to-dev handoff.

5. Developer Collaboration & Design Handoff

Once the high-fidelity designs were finalized for the MVP, I collaborated closely with the development team to ensure seamless implementation. I provided comprehensive design specifications, component behavior documentation, and responsive layout guidelines using Figma's dev-ready features. We conducted regular design reviews and sync meetings to walk through interaction flows, clarify functionality, and resolve edge cases in real time.

Throughout this stage, I remained involved in addressing technical constraints and performance considerations, working with developers to find practical solutions without compromising the core design integrity. We focused on delivering a stable, scalable foundation that supported the app's key features — including itinerary creation, blog discovery, and event submission — across both mobile and web platforms.

It's important to note that this design scope was tailored specifically for the Minimum Viable Product (MVP). The goal was to validate key assumptions and launch with a core set of features to gather real-world user feedback.

Post-launch, based on user interactions and feedback, we identified several areas for enhancement. One major direction for the next iteration is the integration of AI-driven features, particularly within the itinerary builder. The upcoming versions will include conversational AI support, personalized trip planning, smart recommendations, and other intelligent tools to create a more dynamic and context-aware travel assistant experience.

The app is set to launch in September 2025 and can't wait for you to try it! Get ready to experience a smoother, smarter, and more seamless travel journey with Hela.
